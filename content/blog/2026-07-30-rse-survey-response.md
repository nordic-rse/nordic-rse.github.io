72 Research Software Engineering enthusiasts from the Nordics and Baltics answered the international RSE survey in early 2026. The data analysis can be found in XX. This blog bost gives some thoughts to what Nordic-RSE can and will do with the data and a few links to existing resources. 

### Career paths and recognition 

- high satisfaction but unclear promotion routes, weak institutional (vs. supervisor) recognition, and RSE work hiding under inconsistent job titles

-> NRSE can advocate for job title standardization, career ladder models, recognition frameworks. Working together with RSE forum of NeIC. 


### Funding and institutional advocacy

— only 16.7% have access to RSE-specific grants, and "advocating for research software towards politicians/decision makers" was the top-requested institute task

-> NRSE can collect potential grant sources, provide materials for RSEs to advocate for their work within their institution


### Training 

— respondents named specific desired skills: AI-assisted development, HPC/parallel computing, DevOps/CI-CD, testing, communication
-Software sustainability / succession planning — lower priority but a real risk signal: 58.9% say their project would stall if one developer left, only 16.9% have a departure plan 

-> NRSE can gather training landscape and learning paths: https://hackmd.io/@coderefinery/hpc_intro and https://hackmd.io/@coderefinery/RSE_train
-> NRSE can collect resources on software planning (collaborative development, SMP, sunsetting)


### Community

— a large share had to build networks themselves from scratch, and 78.6% of non-members are interested in joining an RSE organisation . Concrete wishes: mentoring, moderated chat, regional meetups.

-> NRSE has a [chat](https://coderefinery.zulipchat.com). If sufficient senior RSEs are willing and available, a buddy system could be set up as a member benefit for NRSE. NRSE is running annual conference, Finland has been running 2 Finnish RSE meetups, needs local volunteers. We can provide guides for organizing. 

### AI usage

- Respondents say that AI is already embedded in day-to-day coding, not yet trusted for design decisions.
- skills gap: critical thinking/output validation, prompt literacy, judgment on when to use it, and foundational SE skills underneath it. "AI-assisted development and agents" was also the top skill people want to develop next. 
— 59.3% expect no significant change to RSE demand, and views on job satisfaction/code quality impact are mixed. 

-> NRSE can link people to HPC through contact to HPC centers and many working in and with HPC (link to HPC training above), encouraging RSEs to be the link to HPC
-> NRSE can connect with Nordic AI factories (LUMI AI Factory, MIMER) and antennas, training/supporting use of AI factory resources, connect RSEs to AI factories for feedback on actual use and needs, maybe a collaborative workshop?

### Connect and join

Come join NRSE, you may consume or collaborate/co-create. We have weekly coffee breaks and bi-weekly meetings as well as separate conference planning meetings.
