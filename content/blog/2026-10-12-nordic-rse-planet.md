Nordic-RSE has a planet now:
[nordic-rse.org/planet](https://nordic-rse.org/planet/)!  Wait,
what...

A "planet" is an old term for a blog aggregator, basically a site that
combines various blogs from other sources into one view.  The point is
to make a way that a community can see what members are up to in a
relatively informal way.  Nordic-RSE has talked about how we can show
our activity better, and this is one of the ideas that came up.

You can read it by looking at the website, or importing the feed into
various different types of feed readers.  You can add your own feed,
and others will be able see updates.  At some point, we'd like to make
post the feed into our Zulipchat.

Planets were popular before big-tech mediated social media platforms
took over.  Given the spirit of Nordic-RSE type people, we thought it
was worth trying it again.  It may require a new news source away from
social media platforms, but perhaps motivating people that direction
is good anyway.

On a personal note, I (rkdarst) think this old style blogging feels
safer and easier somehow.  There is less risk of my posts being
over-amplified, seen by people who don't know my community's context,
or quick negative reactions.  Also, I can subscribe to what I want and
am not algorithmicly fed content decided by someone else.  I don't
want to contribute to the mainstream social media platforms and it's
kind of nice on both the production and consumption sides, so I think
it's worth trying to use old-fashioned tech some.


## How it works

Sites can produce [RSS or Atom
feeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_feed), which are
machine-readable file with recent post content and metadata.  This can
be imported int various "[feed
readers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/News_aggregator)" for users to
view, or re-aggregated in different communities like the old Planet
software used to do.
