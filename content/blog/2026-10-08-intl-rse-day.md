Every year, the second Thursday of October is International RSE Day! 

Today, we celebrate our work and contributions to research, and we share widely our passion for our job. Many in the Nordics and all over the world are celebrating in different ways, from the RSE community in Helsinki meeting for a community lunch, to blog posts, to simple hashtags. Some of us are celebrating simply by enjoying a good cup of coffee together in our weekly [coffee break](https://nordic-rse.org/events/#calendar) taking place every Thursday at 09:00 CE(S)T / 10:00 EE(S)T: everybody is welcome!

Here is to us! Happy International RSE Day!
